Hi, I am currently working on making a remote control roll of filament, (basically a robot disguised as your classic 1KG roll of 3d printer filament). The concept is fairly simple, have two separately driven wheels with a center hub with a very low weight to stay upright (if you need a reference go watch Jurassic park and look at the gyro balls). My original design used the yellow TT motors, but those would have been extremely slow as I wanted it to go at least walking speed (theoretically) meaning I had to switch to higher power motors. The problem with the larger motors is that they would not fit horizontally in the frame, so I added some bevel gears to change the output, and now everything should fit perfectly.  This is my first ever hack club project and I hope I get everything submitted right. Thanks to whoever ends up reading this, Dominic.
