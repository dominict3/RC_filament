# september: started project

completed more CAD. Im building a remote controllable standard roll of filament (basically prophunt). I’m using 2 DC motors being driven by an esp32, most of the parts are suspended in the inner assembly by 2 bearings, allowing them to be free spinning. (basically the gyro balls from Jurassic park).
